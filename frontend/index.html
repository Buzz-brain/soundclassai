About the project

Teaching machines to listen

Environmental Sound Classification uses deep learning to identify everyday sounds — a barking dog, a passing siren, an idling engine.

What is it?

Automatically recognising the source of an everyday sound from a short audio recording.

How AI recognizes sounds

The model maps a recording to a Mel spectrogram — an image of frequencies over time — and learns visual patterns from thousands of examples.

Why Mel Spectrograms

The Mel scale mirrors human hearing — it compresses high frequencies and amplifies the range where our ears (and the sounds we care about) are most sensitive.

Why transfer learning

MobileNetV2 starts pre-trained on millions of images, so the model needs far less data and trains dramatically faster — reaching 85.78% accuracy.

MobileNetV2 CNN · Transfer Learning

A pre-trained MobileNetV2 backbone is adapted for audio: grayscale 224×224 Mel spectrograms flow through the network, ending in a 5-class softmax head.
